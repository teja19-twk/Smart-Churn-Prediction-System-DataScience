# KPI Analysis and Business Metrics

The KPI Analysis phase was performed to measure business performance, customer retention efficiency, and revenue-related insights using the telecom customer churn dataset. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) help organizations monitor customer behavior, identify churn risks, and make data-driven business decisions.

## KPI Calculations

Several important KPIs were calculated using Python and Power BI to evaluate customer retention and churn performance. The KPI calculations were based on customer count, churn values, revenue metrics, and customer lifetime value (CLTV).

The major KPIs analyzed include:

* Total Customers
* Churned Customers
* Retained Customers
* Churn Rate
* Retention Rate
* Total Revenue
* Average Monthly Charges
* Average Tenure
* Average Churn Score
* Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)

## Churn Rate Analysis

Churn Rate measures the percentage of customers who discontinued telecom services during a given period. This KPI is critical for understanding customer loss trends and identifying high-risk customer segments.

The analysis revealed:

* Customers with month-to-month contracts showed significantly higher churn rates.
* Higher monthly charges increased churn probability.
* Lack of technical support and online security services contributed to customer churn.

## Retention Rate Analysis

Retention Rate represents the percentage of customers who continued using telecom services. This metric helps evaluate customer loyalty and service satisfaction.

Key observations:

* Long-term contract customers demonstrated higher retention.
* Customers using bundled services showed better retention behavior.
* Customers with higher tenure months had improved loyalty.

## Revenue Analysis

Revenue-related KPIs were analyzed using Monthly Charges, Total Charges, and CLTV metrics to understand business profitability and customer value.

Important insights:

* Long-term customers generated higher total revenue.
* Customers with higher CLTV contributed significantly to overall business growth.
* Revenue trends helped identify valuable customer segments for retention strategies.

## Visualization and Reporting

KPI metrics were visualized using:

* Retention dashboards

Interactive KPI dashboards were developed using Power BI to support real-time business monitoring and executive-level reporting.
